0. **Before Drafting**:
   - Search his published posts under `astro-build/src/content/` for the claim and its likely metaphors. If he has made the claim before, link that post and say what this one adds.

2. **Name the Reader Payoff**: What should the reader understand or be able to check?
3. **Choose Structure**: Which template from the voice guide fits best?
   - Reflection Post
   - Technical Deep-Dive

   - Origin Story
4. **Draft Following Patterns**:
   - Give the reader a concrete reason to care
   - State the controlling point within 150 words
   - Show the evidence the claim requires
   - Include self-interrogation only when it reflects a real evidentiary turn
   - Use bold headers
   - Complete the answer before leaving any uncertainty open
5. **Create Frontmatter**: Generate proper metadata, including `takeaway`
6. **Review Against Checklist**: Verify evidence, scope, reader payoff, then voice authenticity
   - Check the point against his own starting words, not only against a reader's summary of the draft. A reviewer who sees only the draft can confirm that it has a point, not that the point is his.
   - Before each handover, run reader-clarity's continuity and concrete-subject tests over the whole draft, not only the sentences he quoted.
   - After a full rewrite, check the previous review's flagged lines against the new text, because a rewrite can bring them back.

Do not add questions, fragments, counts, session chronology, or a counterpoint only to satisfy a corpus pattern. Apart from approving the point, ask clarifying questions only when missing evidence or intent would materially change the result. Show examples from the voice guide when helpful, without copying their phrasing.
