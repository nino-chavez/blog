### Candidate A

Easiest: enter through the newest essay, understand its premise quickly, then continue through a compact chronological reading list. The homepage has a strong editorial rhythm.

Hardest: browse by a specific interest or re-orient after landing directly on an essay. `a-essay-desktop.png` and `a-essay-phone.png` show only an “All essays” return link; the publication name and wider navigation are absent from the visible reading frame. The food image is handsome but provides little topical signal for an essay about AI, agency, and volleyball.

### Candidate B

Easiest: find a known essay and browse a large chronological archive. The persistent essay list, search field, and fully branded essay view make the archive feel practical and the reading page well oriented.

Hardest: understand what the publication is before beginning to search. `b-home-desktop.png` and `b-home-phone.png` visibly begin as an “Essays” utility/index, without the Signal Dispatch name, global navigation, or a concise statement of the publication’s point of view. It feels like an internal library entrance rather than a public writing site’s front door.

### Candidate C

Easiest: explore by subject and choose from a visually legible, varied set of writing. The homepage gives the clearest sense of range and makes the site feel like an active publication rather than a single feed.

Hardest: establish freshness and preserve publication context during reading. `c-home-desktop.png` and `c-home-phone.png` give categories and read times but no visible dates on the cards, making recency harder to assess. `c-essay-desktop.png` and especially `c-essay-phone.png` remove the Signal Dispatch masthead and visible route back to the archive from the initial reading frame. On phone, the dense two-line subject link cluster in `c-home-phone.png` is readable but less calm than the rest of the page.

### Preferred: Candidate B

B is the strongest overall choice because the essay is the product’s most consequential frame, and B makes a direct reader arrival feel unquestionably located: Signal Dispatch, the archive, navigation, title, author, date, and reading time are all present in `b-essay-desktop.png` and `b-essay-phone.png`. Its front page needs a more editorial introduction, but that is a smaller correction than restoring orientation to every shared essay link.

Retain from A: its “Latest essay” lead and “More to read” digest; they make a public-facing home feel current and human.

Retain from C: its subject-led exploration and image-rich card grid; they make the archive easier to enter when a reader has an interest rather than a known title.
