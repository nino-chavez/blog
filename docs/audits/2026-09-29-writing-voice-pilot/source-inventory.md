Observed behaviors, with low confidence as cross-format claims:

- Short replies often rely on shared context. Their brevity would not make a cold reader understand an essay.
- Some longer replies supply concrete background before a conditional offer or practical next step.
- Humor can arrive through a shared image or link and a small comment; the preview's prose is not Nino's writing.
- The additional selected group mainly supplied short encouragement, questions, and link exchanges in the viewed portion. It contributes little evidence about sustained argument.

These observations guide future sampling. They have not been promoted to drafting rules or passed as private examples to either essay writer. No messages, reactions, or invitations were sent. The phone was returned to its home screen after sampling.

### Observations grounded in this document

| Observation | Page evidence | What it contributes; what it does not |
|---|---|---|
| Define a term, explain what the mechanism does, then explain why it matters | Page 6 introduces static data, distinguishes normalization from enrichment, gives examples, and explains the downstream consequence | Evidence of a sustained explanatory sequence. It does not require every paragraph to follow that sequence. |
| Narrow a claim at the point where a reader might misread it | Page 6 qualifies sameness as structure and type, “not content/values”; page 4 narrows “Manage” to “view” | The parenthetical performs a precision job. Parentheses themselves are not a quota or a signature to manufacture. |
| Walk the reader through a changing state | Page 10 explains the first lookup, the value at that moment, the changed value, and what later lookups receive | Concrete sequence and consequence, rather than a summary that skips the mechanism. No system-specific facts belong in the AI-assistants essay. |
| Attach uncertainty to unresolved design choices | Page 3 explicitly declares the proposal incomplete; pages 13–15 distinguish established behavior from possible changes | Supports separating settled facts from proposals. It does not establish a personal hedge rate; the drafting stage itself explains much of this wording. |
| Allow personality inside formal work | Page 4 uses a playful aside in required reading and a prominent simplicity principle | Counterevidence to a universal ban on humor or emphatic short lines. These phrases must not become imitation templates. |
| Give explanation room when the relationship needs it | Pages 6 and 10 use connected multi-sentence paragraphs with technical terms intact | This sample does not support reducing Nino's prose to terse chat-like sentences. It also does not establish that longer sentences are generally better. |

The most useful working hypothesis is that the reader should be able to follow why something works or follows, with the likely misunderstanding handled nearby. That is a hypothesis for later comparison, not a new canonical voice rule. The sample contains both extended explanation and compressed emphasis; that combination is evidence against treating either length alone or a rhetorical device as an authenticity detector.
