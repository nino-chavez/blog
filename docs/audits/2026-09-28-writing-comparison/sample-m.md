I asked a coding agent to help build a photo editor that could learn how I edit. By the end, we had working Lightroom automation, trained prediction models, and a way to compare their results without knowing which method made each image.

We still had no demonstrated advantage over a fixed recipe. No measured editing time saved.

The main coding conversation had consumed roughly $795 in API-equivalent tokens. That is an estimate at standard prices, not a bill I received. It also leaves out separately dispatched workers and the photo models' own API calls.

The failure was continuing to fund technical progress without requiring it to justify the next experiment. The changes I want are concrete: account for the whole task, compare against the workflow I already use, and let a negative result end an assignment.

I photograph volleyball. In Lightroom, I already apply presets and then adjust the photographs that need more work. A preset is a saved collection of edits. An assistant would need to do something more useful: adapt those edits to each photograph well enough to reduce my finishing work.

In a ten-photo comparison, I preferred the learned adjustments zero times. They lost six comparisons and tied four against a fixed recipe derived from the training edits. A simpler learned model then lost seven and tied three on those same photos.

These were small development tests. The photographs overlapped. The full workflow comparison remains unmeasured.

During the session, I wrote: “development time and effort is negligible since it is all being done through coding agents and harnesses.”

The usage record puts a limit on that claim. Delegating the work moved its cost; it did not eliminate it.

The research plan proposed a $100 compute ceiling for inference, training, and diffusion experiments. That ceiling did not include the conversation designing, building, checking, and supervising them.

The handoff needs to retain the decision, failed approaches, evidence, and remaining budget. The conversation can reset. The allowance cannot.

I have not installed these new controls. The current outcome is narrower: the predictor configuration is stopped, its useful tooling is retained, and there is a plan for how another investigation should be bounded.

I already had a preset. The assistant still has to earn the extra step.
