TITLE: One capture, two ways to teach the product
---
Rally HQ's product walkthroughs now have two useful forms: a guide someone can move through at their own pace, and a video of the same journey.

Agents capture the real interface one step at a time. The frames, explanations and target areas go into one shared file. Render Kit uses that source to produce both outputs, so a revised explanation does not need to be recreated in a second edit.

The new demonstration includes the tournament guide and its matching video. It also shows how the organizer, captain and fan journeys share their public labels across the product's homepage and demo gallery.

These are staged product examples, not customer records. They demonstrate a working way to produce teaching material. Whether they improve learning or save production time has not been measured.
